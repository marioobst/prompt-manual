# Design and editorial decisions

Status: 14 November 2026.

## A reference, not a course

Readers choose their own starting point. Short pages answer distinct questions; related links help connect ideas without imposing a sequence. Decorative chapter numbers were removed to avoid suggesting mandatory stages.

## Expressive entrance, calm reading

The homepage uses PROMPT MANUAL as its main visual focus. Violet, lime and muted grey-lilac give the project a distinctive identity. Article pages use a quieter layout, compact sections and clear headings. The logo combines the ideas of a manual and a conversation and also serves as the favicon.

## Orientation while reading

A horizontal reading-progress indicator and an active section marker help readers keep their place. Section links allow a specific passage to be bookmarked or shared. The layout adapts to desktop and smartphone screens.

## English and German

English is the default homepage. German content has its own URLs, and the language switch opens the corresponding page. Both versions are maintained as part of the same project.

## Privacy by Design

The website serves its images, icons and styling locally and embeds no third-party services. Optional analytics are not used. External destinations open in a new tab. The website runs on Cloudflare Workers; necessary technical processing is explained in its privacy policy.

## Learning by doing

Mario and Aik develop ideas through conversation, examples, comparison and revision. Mario makes publication decisions and takes responsibility for the content. AI assistance does not replace independent checking.

Dated draft labels are intentional: they make ongoing work visible and invite readers to try ideas and offer feedback. Illustrations of Mario and Aik are identified as artistic AI illustrations.
